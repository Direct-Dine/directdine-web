About Us

More Than Coffee —
It's a Feeling.

Sunny Street started with a simple belief: everyone deserves a space that feels like home. We roast our own beans, bake pastries at dawn, and keep our menu honest.

Whether you need a quiet corner or a table for six — our doors are open.

  • House-roasted single-origin coffee
  • Vegan & gluten-free options daily
  • Kid-friendly seating & menu
  • Free Wi-Fi, all day
