Our Story

A Legacy Brewed with Love

◆

In 1989, Madeleine Duval set out to recreate the warmth of a Parisian corner café in the heart of the city. Armed with a recipe book passed down from her grandmother and an unwavering belief that coffee should be a ritual, not a rush — Bon Bouquet was born.

Three decades on, we honour that vision every single morning. Our beans are sourced directly from family farms. Our pastries are made at dawn. And our tables are always set for those who need a moment of beauty.
